Shortcuts

Click
Place the cursor. Click the same cell again to flip direction.
Tab / Enter
Flip between across and down.
↑↓←→
Move the cursor; sets the direction to match.
Space
Clear the cell and step forward.
⌫
Step back and clear.
Drag / ⇧+Click
Select a block of cells.
⌫ on a selection
Clear those cells.
⌘+V
Paste a word — or a whole multi-line layout.
⌘+Z
Undo. ⇧+⌘+Z to redo.
Esc
Drop the selection.
